# Domain Glossary

Each term is defined exactly once, here. Every other document in `docs/domain/` and every future
document must use these definitions consistently rather than restating or varying them.

| Term                   | Definition                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| ---------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Clinic**             | The tenant: a medical clinic account on the platform. Owns its own Services, Staff Members, Patients, Conversations, Appointments, and Knowledge Documents, and no other Clinic's data.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| **Patient**            | A record representing a person who has messaged or been booked with a Clinic. Never an authenticated user of the system — has no login, password, or session (charter §7).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| **Appointment**        | The entity: a specific Patient scheduled for a specific Service with a specific Practitioner at a specific TimeSlot. Persists with its own identity from creation until cancelled or completed.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| **Booking**            | The _act_ of creating an Appointment — whether performed directly by staff from the dashboard, or by the Assistant on a Patient's behalf during a Conversation. Not a separate entity: every Booking results in exactly one Appointment, and this model tracks the Appointment, not the act that produced it.                                                                                                                                                                           |
| **Conversation**       | A Patient's message thread with a Clinic, handled by the Assistant, staff, or both in sequence. Contains Messages and, when applicable, Escalations.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| **Knowledge Document** | A document a Clinic uploads that the Assistant may draw on to answer administrative questions, once its status is Ready.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| **Staff Member**       | An authenticated person working for a Clinic, with exactly one Role: owner, admin, practitioner, or receptionist (`docs/adr/0004-staff-role-model.md`).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| **Escalation**         | A recorded instance of a Conversation being handed from the Assistant to staff, with a reason (clinical question, medical emergency, ungroundable answer, or explicit patient request for a human) and a history of who acknowledged and closed it.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| **Tenant**             | The abstract isolation boundary this platform enforces so that one Clinic's data is never visible to another (`docs/adr/0003-multi-tenancy-model.md`). In this domain, exactly one Tenant exists per Clinic — the two terms describe the same thing from two angles: "Clinic" is the business entity, "Tenant" is the isolation boundary that entity defines. `06-multi-tenancy.md` uses "Tenant" when discussing isolation mechanics specifically; every other document says "Clinic." |
| **Aggregate**          | A cluster of one or more entities and value objects that must stay consistent with each other, and that other code only ever reaches through its Aggregate Root.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| **Aggregate Root**     | The single entity within an Aggregate that everything outside the Aggregate is allowed to reference or modify through. Internal entities of the aggregate (e.g., a Conversation's Messages) have no meaning or access path independent of their root.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| **Entity**             | Something with identity that persists across changes to its attributes — two Entities with identical attributes are still two different things, distinguished by identity, not by value.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| **Value Object**       | Something defined entirely by its attributes, with no identity of its own — two Value Objects with equal attributes are interchangeable, and neither is "the original."                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
