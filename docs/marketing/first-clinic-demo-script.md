# First-clinic demo script (5–7 minutes)

**Status:** draft for the Owner's review. Synthetic data only: no real patient, real clinic
document or real credential is shown or entered.

**Where to demo.** Use the demo environment (`clinic-ai-demo`), with its fictional clinic, the
on-screen "non-production environment" notice, and the Owner operating the device. The visitor
never receives a link, an account or a screen to type into. Showing the demo to anyone outside the
project needs the Owner's separate approval first
(`docs/operations/2026-10-21-demo-environment-option-c.md`, rule 3). Never demo in production.

**If the demo cannot be shown** (no approval, no connection, or the service is asleep on the Free
plan), use screenshots taken from the demo beforehand and say so: "These are screenshots from our
test environment." Never present a screenshot or a mock-up as a live system.

**The screens are in Arabic only.** No Greek or English staff interface exists yet. Explain each
screen out loud as you show it, and say plainly that the Greek interface is still to be built.

**The demo has no file storage.** Uploading a knowledge document fails there, so show the
knowledge-base screen and explain it. Do not attempt an upload.

## 0. Before the meeting

- [ ] Owner approval for an external demo is recorded.
- [ ] The demo service is awake: open it 2 minutes before, because the Free plan sleeps.
- [ ] The fictional clinic has a few invented conversations and one practitioner with working hours.
- [ ] Screenshots are ready as a fallback.
- [ ] The questions from section 4 are printed or on the phone.

## 1. Opening (1 minute)

> "Thank you for your time. I am Ahmed. I am building a tool for the front desk of dental clinics.
> This is not a sales meeting. I want to understand how your clinic works and whether this would
> help you at all. What I show you uses a fictional clinic and invented data."

## 2. What to show (2–3 minutes): only what works today

1. **Inbox.** The conversation list of the fictional clinic. "Your staff see every patient
   conversation in one place."
2. **A conversation and a staff reply.** Type a reply as staff. "The reply is written by your
   staff."
3. **Book from the conversation.** Pick a practitioner and a free slot, then try the same slot
   again to show that double-booking is refused. "The system prevents two appointments in the same
   slot."
4. **Knowledge-base screen.** The list of clinic documents. "Here the clinic keeps its price list,
   opening hours and policies." In this environment, do not upload.
5. **Staff and roles.** The staff list and invitations. "Each person sees only what their role
   allows, and each clinic's data is separated from every other clinic's."

## 3. What is not built yet (1 minute). Say it plainly.

> "Three things are not built yet, and I want your opinion on them. First, the screens are not in
> Greek yet. Second, patients cannot yet write in from your website, WhatsApp or Viber. In this
> demo, the messages are invented. Third, there are no automatic answers yet. The plan is that the system drafts an answer only from your own
> documents, and your staff approve it before it is sent. It will never give medical advice."

Use this rule throughout: **works today** means you showed it on screen. Anything else is a
**plan** or an **idea**. Use those exact words.

## 4. Questions for the clinic owner (2 minutes)

1. How do patients contact you today: phone, Viber, WhatsApp, email, your website, a booking site?
2. Roughly how many calls or messages go unanswered in a week, for example during treatment or
   after hours?
3. Which questions repeat most (prices, hours, availability, directions)?
4. Do you have patients from abroad? Which languages?
5. Which software do you use for appointments and patient records today?
6. What would make you trust an assistant that drafts replies? What would make you refuse it?
7. Would you try it for free for 2–3 months and give feedback every two weeks?

Write down their own words. Do not argue with an answer.

## 5. Close (1 minute)

> "Thank you, this helps a lot. May I come back in a few weeks to show you what changed based on
> what you told me? And may I send you a short summary of today, to check that I understood you
> correctly?"

Do not promise dates, features or prices. Do not sign anything at the first meeting.

## After the meeting

- Within 24 hours: a short thank-you with the summary, sent only after the Owner approves it.
- Record the answers in an internal note, with no patient data and nothing the clinic did not agree
  to share.
